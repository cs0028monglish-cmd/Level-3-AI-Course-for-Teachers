What an interactive book needs

This is objective 2. Read the list first, then hold the model book on this page against it — every item below is somewhere in that book.

  • Read to methe page reads itself aloud at a speed you set, lighting up each word as it is spoken.
  • Your own recorded voicerecord narration for a page in the book, or upload a file — the child hears their teacher, not a machine.
  • Background musicone quiet track under the whole book, with its own volume, low enough to read over.
  • A picture on every pageand the picture prompt written beside it, so the illustration is generated to order instead of hunted for.
  • Pages that really turna page-turn, not a scroll. A book should behave like a book, on a board and on a phone.
  • A question where the text ismultiple choice, true or false, matching and open — asked on the page, not collected at the end.
  • Feedback that gives a reasonnot “wrong”, but which clue in the text answers it — and an open question saved for the teacher, never marked wrong.
  • Marking tools for the childhighlighters and an eraser, so the learner marks the words they are working on.
  • A teacher's note on any pagea reminder for the next lesson, or for the next teacher who opens the book.
  • Reading settingstext size, a dyslexia-friendly font, high contrast and reduced motion — accessibility is a feature, not an extra.
  • A cover with a real titlethe title sits on the artwork, placed and sized by you, so the book looks like a book from the first screen.
  • A student copyreader mode hides the workshop, and Present fills the class board — the child gets the book, not the tools.
  • Print and PDFthe same book on paper, questions included, for a class with no devices that day.
  • Links and embedsa video, a song or an activity living inside a page instead of beside the lesson.
  • One offline filedownload a single .html the child opens with no internet, no login and no app — and it saves itself while you work.

The test for any book tool: can the child open it alone, hear it, answer inside it, and keep it without an account? A tool that fails the last part gives you a demo, not a book you own.

Five things to do in the book above

Do them now, in the frame, before we look at the template. This is objective 3 in practice.

  1. Turn the pagePress Next, or the arrow keys. Press Back once to see the cover, with its title sitting on the artwork.
  2. Make it read aloudOn page 1 press ▶ Read to me and watch each word light up as it is spoken.
  3. Answer all four kinds of questionPage 1 and 2 are multiple choice, page 3 is matching, page 4 is open. Read the feedback, not only the colour.
  4. Put your own voice on a pagePress ♫ Audio, choose Page 1, record two lines, stop, and press play in the studio. Then close it and press 🔊 Narration on the page.
  5. Hand it to a learnerPress Reader mode, then Present for the class board, then Download book for the one offline file.

Notice the questions: each page uses a different type on purpose, and the ladder runs recall → inference → vocabulary → evaluation. The open question on page 4 is never marked wrong; it is saved for you to read.

Objective 4 · Open the template and personalise it

One template, your story inside it.

You do not rebuild the book. You replace its story block, and everything else — the page turn, the reading voice, the questions, the download — stays exactly as it is.

Five steps, about twenty minutes

  1. Open the book above and press Change storyThe setup card appears with an empty box and the step “Copy your story and paste it below”.
  2. Ask ChatGPT or Gemini for your storyGive it your topic, CEFR level, main character, number of pages and the language of the book — and ask for the story block only, with no explanation around it.
  3. Paste it and press Load storyThe book opens with your text on every page and a picture prompt waiting beside each one.
  4. Make the picturesOn each page press Copy prompt, generate the picture in Gemini or Canva, then press ＋ Add picture.
  5. Record your voice, then downloadOpen ♫ Audio, record the narration page by page, then press Download book for one offline file to hand to the class.

If the block will not load: the tool is telling you the JSON is broken, not that your story is wrong. Ask the AI to “return the same story as one valid JSON object only, no fences and no explanation”, and paste again.

Objective 5 · Check Canva's option

Canva makes the pages. It does not make the book.

This is worth checking honestly, because many teachers already live in Canva. The answer is not “no” — it is “yes, for one half of the job”.

Use Canva for this

  • The illustrations and the cover Canva's image tools, elements and layouts are a fast, familiar way to make or finish the picture for each page.
  • Page art and typography If you want a designed page rather than a plain one, lay it out in Canva and export it as the page picture.
  • A quick printed version Canva exports a clean print PDF, which is useful for the paper copy of a book you built elsewhere.
  • A small activity with Canva Code Canva Code generates interactive activities from a description — a quiz, matching, flashcards. An activity, not a whole book.

Do not expect this from Canva

  • No page-turning book of its own Canva has no native flipbook. You send the design to a third-party app in the Apps panel — FlippingBook, Flipsnack — and the book is published on that company's platform as a link.
  • No “Read to me” Nothing reads the page aloud to the child and highlights the words.
  • No recorded narration per page There is no place for the teacher's own voice on each page of a published flipbook.
  • No question on the page No multiple choice, matching or open question with feedback and a score inside the book.
  • Not one offline file The flipbook lives on someone else's servers behind a link, and needs the internet and an account.

The decision: draw in Canva, build in the template. Export each page picture from Canva, then press ＋ Add picture in the book. You get Canva's design and the book's voice, questions and offline file — instead of choosing between them.
